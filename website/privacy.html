SoccerCoach: 7v7 Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

SoccerCoach does not collect, transmit, or share any of your information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers. This page explains exactly what the app stores and where.

1. Information the app stores

SoccerCoach stores the coaching content you create directly in the app, such as:

  • Player names, jersey numbers, and playable positions
  • Lineups, formations, and substitution plans
  • Fair-play minutes and saved game reports
  • Practice plans, drills, notes, and any photos you attach to a drill

2. Where it's stored

All of this content is stored locally on your iPhone, in the app's private storage area. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing is shared with the developer or any third party. If you attach a photo to a drill, the app only accesses the specific photo you choose through the iPhone's built-in photo picker, and that photo stays on your device.

3. No accounts, no tracking

SoccerCoach has no sign-in, no user accounts, no subscriptions, and no analytics or advertising tracking of any kind.

4. Data retention and deletion

Information you create in the app remains on your device until you delete it inside the app, delete the app, or clear the app's data through iOS device settings. Since nothing is stored anywhere else, deleting the app permanently removes all of your data.

5. Children's privacy

SoccerCoach is intended for coaches and other adults managing a youth soccer team. The app is not directed to children as users, and it does not knowingly collect information from children, since it does not collect any information at all.

6. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the support page.
